My Love For Coffee

Coffee is a big part of my life. I worked as a barista for many years, first at Starbucks and later at Pret in London. Every morning I made hundreds of coffees for people going to work. It was busy and sometimes stressful, but I really enjoyed it.

Working as a barista taught me a lot. I learned how to steam milk, how to make latte art, and how small changes in grind size can change the taste of an espresso. I also learned how to talk to customers and remember their favourite drinks.

Today I don’t work as a barista anymore, but I still love coffee. I like trying new coffee shops in London and comparing their flat whites. For me, a good cup of coffee is the best way to start the day.

My Favourite Coffee Drinks

  • Flat white
  • Cappuccino
  • Espresso
  • Iced Latte
  • Turkish Coffee
